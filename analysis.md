Analysis
Layer 8, Head 10

This head appears to link direct objects to the verbs they belong to. In each sentence, the object noun attends most strongly to the verb that acts on it, rather than to [SEP] or [CLS].

Example Sentences:

She quietly closed the [MASK] behind her.
The chef carefully prepared the [MASK] for the guests.
He kicked the [MASK] across the field.
Layer 8, Head 11

This head appears to link determiners to the nouns they modify. Words like "the" and "a" attend most strongly to the noun that follows them.

Example Sentences:

The old [MASK] stood at the end of the street.
A small dog chased the [MASK] around the garden.
Every student finished the [MASK] before noon.
